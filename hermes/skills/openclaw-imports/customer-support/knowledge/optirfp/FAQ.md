## Common FAQs

### Getting Started
**Q: How do I create my first RFP?**
A: Click "New RFP" in the dashboard, upload your document or paste text, and our AI will analyze and structure it. Watch our 5-minute onboarding video in the Help Center.

**Q: Can I import existing RFPs?**
A: Yes! We support PDF, Word, and text imports. The AI will parse and organize existing content.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes, 14-day free trial with full Professional features. No credit card required.

### Technical Issues
**Q: I can't log in**
A: Try resetting your password. If issues persist, check if your account is active or contact support.

**Q: The AI isn't generating good responses**
A: Try providing more context about your company and past proposals. The AI learns from your specific writing style and requirements.

**Q: My document won't upload**
A: We support PDF, DOCX, and TXT files up to 50MB. Ensure your file isn't password-protected or corrupted.

**Q: The platform is slow/unresponsive**
A: Clear your browser cache, try a different browser, or check your internet connection. Enterprise customers can contact us for performance optimization.

### Billing & Account
**Q: How do I upgrade/downgrade my plan?**
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: We offer refunds within 14 days of purchase if you're not satisfied. Contact support to request.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

**Q: Do you offer annual billing?**
A: Yes! Annual billing saves 20%. Contact sales for enterprise annual contracts.

### Features & Usage
**Q: How does the AI writing assistant work?**
A: Our AI analyzes your RFP requirements and generates tailored responses based on your company profile and past submissions. You can edit and refine as needed.

**Q: Can multiple team members collaborate?**
A: Yes, Professional and Enterprise plans support team collaboration with role-based permissions.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption, SOC 2 compliance, and your data is never used to train our AI models.

**Q: Do you integrate with other tools?**
A: We integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Drive, Dropbox, and offer API access for custom integrations (Enterprise).

### Training & Support
**Q: Do you offer training?**
A: Yes! We have video tutorials, documentation, and live webinars. Enterprise customers get personalized onboarding.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Starter: Email support (24-48 hour response). Professional/Enterprise: Priority email and chat support.

**Q: Can I request a feature?**
A: Yes! Submit feature requests through the dashboard or contact your account manager (Enterprise).
