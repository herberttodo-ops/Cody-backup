## Common FAQs

### Getting Started
**Q: How do I request a quote?**
A: Fill out the project inquiry form on our website or email andrew@rivalproductions.com with your project details, timeline, and budget.

**Q: What information do you need for a quote?**
A: Project type, estimated length, shoot location(s), timeline, deliverables needed, and any specific requirements or examples you like.

**Q: How far in advance should I book?**
A: Ideally 4-6 weeks for standard projects. Rush projects can sometimes be accommodated with a rush fee.

### Pricing & Billing
**Q: How much does a video cost?**
A: Prices vary widely based on scope. Simple projects start around $2,500. Full-scale productions can range $10K-$50K+. We provide custom quotes for every project.

**Q: What is your payment structure?**
A: 50% deposit to book, 50% upon final delivery. Net 15 terms available for established clients.

**Q: Are there any additional fees?**
A: Travel outside Philadelphia area, special equipment rentals, talent/location fees, and music licensing are quoted separately.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I cancel?**
A: Deposits are refundable if cancelled 14+ days before shoot date. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit deposit.

### Production Process
**Q: How long does production take?**
A: Typical timeline: 2-4 weeks from shoot to final delivery, depending on complexity. Rush delivery available for additional fee.

**Q: Can I be involved in the editing process?**
A: Absolutely! We include 2 rounds of revisions in every project. Additional revisions available at $150/hour.

**Q: Do you provide scripts or storyboards?**
A: We can develop scripts and storyboards as part of pre-production services. Let us know your needs in the initial consultation.

**Q: What if I need changes after the project is complete?**
A: Minor edits (text changes, logo swaps) are billed at $150/hour. Major changes are quoted separately.

### Technical Specifications
**Q: What resolution do you deliver in?**
A: Standard delivery is 4K or 1080p depending on project needs. We can deliver in multiple formats.

**Q: What file formats do you deliver?**
A: MP4 (H.264) is standard. We can also provide ProRes, MOV, or other formats upon request.

**Q: Do you provide raw footage?**
A: Raw footage is available for an additional fee, subject to a usage agreement.

**Q: Who owns the final video?**
A: You own full rights to the final edited video. We retain the right to use it in our portfolio unless otherwise agreed.

### Webinar Production
**Q: Do you handle the technical side of webinars?**
A: Yes! We manage platform setup, testing, live technical support, recording, and post-production.

**Q: Can you help with slides or graphics?**
A: Yes, we offer presentation design and motion graphics services for webinars.

**Q: What platforms do you work with?**
A: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, GoToWebinar, Hopin, and custom platforms.

**Q: Do you provide rehearsal time?**
A: Yes, we include a tech check/rehearsal session before every live webinar.
