# TLC Rescue - Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Business Overview
Dog rescue organization focused on saving and rehoming dogs in need. Funded through donations, events, and sponsorships.

- Monthly recurring donations
- Memorial/honorarium donations
- Corporate sponsorships

### Events
- Bingo Unleashed (annual fundraiser)
- Adoption events

- Community outreach events

### Volunteering
- Dog walking
- Fostering

- Transport assistance

## Common FAQs

### Donations
**Q: How do I make a donation?**
A: You can donate online at tlcrescue.org/donate, send a check to our mailing address, or donate in person at events. All donations are tax-deductible (EIN: XX-XXXXXXX).

**Q: Is my donation tax-deductible?**
A: Yes! TLC Rescue is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. You'll receive a receipt for tax purposes.

**Q: Can I donate supplies instead of money?**
A: Absolutely! We always need dog food, treats, blankets, toys, cleaning supplies, and gift cards to pet stores. Contact us for our current needs list.

**Q: How are donations used?**
A: 100% of donations go directly to animal care: veterinary bills, food, shelter, and rescue operations. Administrative costs are covered by specific grants.

**Q: Can I donate in memory of someone?**
A: Yes, memorial donations are a beautiful way to honor a loved one (human or pet). We'll send a notification card to the family.

### Events
**Q: When is the next Bingo Unleashed?**
A: Check our website or follow us on social media for upcoming dates. It's our biggest annual fundraiser!

**Q: How do I buy tickets for Bingo Unleashed?**
A: Tickets are available online at tlcrescue.org/events or at the door (subject to availability).

**Q: Can I sponsor an event?**
A: Yes! We have sponsorship tiers from $250 to $5,000+. Contact fundraising@tlcrescue.org for a sponsorship packet.

**Q: Are events family-friendly?**
A: Yes! Bingo Unleashed is fun for all ages. Some events may be 21+ (noted in event details).

### Volunteering
**Q: How do I become a volunteer?**
A: Fill out the volunteer application on our website and attend a volunteer orientation (held monthly).

**Q: Do I need experience to volunteer?**
A: No experience needed for most roles! We'll train you. Some roles (like medical fostering) require specific experience.

**Q: How much time do I need to commit?**
A: As little or as much as you can! Some volunteers help at one event per year, others volunteer weekly.

**Q: Can I volunteer with my kids?**
A: Yes, many opportunities are family-friendly. Volunteers under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

### Adoption
**Q: How do I adopt a dog?**
A: Browse available dogs on our website, fill out an application, and our adoption coordinator will contact you within 48 hours.

**Q: What is the adoption fee?**
A: Adoption fees vary by age/breed ($150-$400) and include spay/neuter, vaccinations, and microchip.

**Q: Can I foster instead of adopt?**
A: Yes! Fostering is crucial to our mission. We provide all supplies and medical care; you provide the love.
