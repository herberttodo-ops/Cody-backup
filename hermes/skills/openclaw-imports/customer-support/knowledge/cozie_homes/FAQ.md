# Cozie Homes - Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Business Overview
Short-term rental property management in the Poconos, Pennsylvania.

## Properties
- Multiple STR properties in Poconos region
- Focus on family-friendly, cozy getaways
- Amenities vary by property

## Common FAQs

### Booking & Reservations
**Q: How do I book a property?**
A: You can book directly through our website or through Airbnb/VRBO. Direct bookings receive a 10% discount.

**Q: What is your cancellation policy?**
A: Full refund if cancelled 14+ days before check-in. 50% refund if cancelled 7-14 days. No refund if cancelled less than 7 days.

**Q: Can I modify my reservation dates?**
A: Yes, if the property is available for your new dates. Date changes within 7 days of check-in may incur a fee.

### Check-in & Check-out
**Q: What time is check-in/check-out?**
A: Check-in is 4:00 PM, check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available by request.

**Q: How do I get the door code?**
A: Door codes are sent via text and email 24 hours before check-in.

**Q: What if I arrive late at night?**
A: No problem! Our properties have self check-in. The door code works 24/7.

### Amenities
**Q: Are linens and towels provided?**
A: Yes, all properties include fresh linens, towels, and basic toiletries.

**Q: Is WiFi included?**
A: Yes, all properties have free high-speed WiFi. Details are in the welcome guide.

**Q: Are pets allowed?**
A: Some properties are pet-friendly (noted in listing). Pet fee is $50 per stay.

**Q: Is there a fireplace?**
A: Many properties have fireplaces or fire pits. Check the specific listing for details.

### During Your Stay
**Q: Something is broken/not working. What do I do?**
A: Text or call our support line immediately. We aim to respond within 1 hour during business hours (8 AM - 8 PM).

**Q: Can I have visitors during my stay?**
A: Day visitors are fine. Overnight guests must be declared and may incur additional charges.

**Q: Is smoking allowed?**
A: All properties are non-smoking. Smoking outside is fine, please dispose of butts properly.

### Issues & Problems
**Q: I left something at the property**
A: We can mail lost items for a $15 shipping fee plus postage. Unclaimed items are donated after 30 days.

**Q: The property is not as described**
A: We want you to be happy! Contact us within 24 hours of check-in and we'll make it right or offer a partial refund.

**Q: There's a cleanliness issue**
A: We apologize! Please send photos and we'll arrange a re-clean or provide a refund for cleaning fees.
