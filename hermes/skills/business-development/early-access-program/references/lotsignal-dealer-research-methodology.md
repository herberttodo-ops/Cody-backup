# LotSignal Dealer Research Methodology
## Early Access Program Case Study

**Product:** LotSignal - Aged inventory marketing automation for car dealerships  
**ICP:** Single-point franchise dealers (Ford, Chevy, Dodge/Jeep, Nissan)  
**Target Geography:** Desert heat markets (AZ, TX), seasonal markets (Northeast, Midwest)  
**Target Size:** $30-80M annual revenue (decision makers accessible)

---

## Research Framework

### Selection Criteria (Weighted Scorecard)

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Pain Level | HIGH | Inventory aging challenges visible (heat, seasonal, competition) |
| Accessibility | HIGH | GM/Owner reachable on LinkedIn |
| Speed to Value | MEDIUM | Can see results within 30 days |
| Willingness to Pay | MEDIUM | Has marketing budget, understands carrying costs |

### Market Types & Pain Angles

1. **Desert Heat Markets** (Phoenix, El Paso, West Texas)
   - Pain: 110°+ days age inventory 2x faster
   - Angle: "Sky Harbor heat corridor" / visible depreciation
   - Keywords: "rapid aging," "lot constraints," "heat damage"

2. **Seasonal Markets** (Northeast, Upper Midwest)
   - Pain: Winter prep creates annual aged inventory rush
   - Angle: "Fall aging surge before first snow"
   - Keywords: "seasonal patterns," "winter damage," "salt corrosion"

3. **Ag/Economy Cycles** (Rural TX, MO, KS)
   - Pain: Commodity price volatility affects truck demand overnight
   - Angle: "When commodity prices shift, your inventory strategy must too"
   - Keywords: "agricultural cycles," "boom-bust," "commodity prices"

4. **Competitive Metros** (DFW, Philly, Atlanta)
   - Pain: 20+ dealers in market, aged inventory bleeds margin fast
   - Angle: "Hyper-competition requires hyper-targeted marketing"
   - Keywords: "metro competition," "carrying costs," "price pressure"

## 4-Message Outreach Sequence

### Day 1: Connection Request
```
Hi [Name], saw you're running [Dealer]. Quick question about how you handle [market-specific challenge] in [Region].

Not selling anything, just researching how [segment] dealers are approaching this.

-Andrew
```

### Day 2: Follow-Up After Accept
```
Thanks for connecting [Name].

Been talking to [Region] dealers and hearing a consistent theme:

"[Representative pain quote]"

Does that resonate with your situation or do you have a different approach?
```

### Day 4: Specific Pain Focus
```
[Name], specific scenario:

How many vehicles sitting 60+ days without a dedicated marketing campaign this week?

Most [Region] dealers: "Probably 30+"

That gap shows up in monthly P&L whether you track it or not.

Is that [market-specific challenge] affecting you or do you have a system to stay ahead of it?
```

### Day 7: Soft Pitch
```
[Name], built something that might help with aged inventory visibility.

LotSignal does daily scans of your inventory, flags aged units, and pre-drafts targeted campaigns.

Built specifically for [Region] dealers dealing with [market challenge].

Might fit [Dealer] or might not. Worth a 10-min call to see if it addresses what you're seeing?
```

## Key Lessons

- **Market-specific pain angles** outperform generic messaging (desert heat vs seasonal vs ag cycles)
- **Tiered approach** with 4-message sequence works better than cold pitch
- **Hybrid research** — agent builds framework, user executes LinkedIn lookups
- **Building in public** strategy documents real journey, builds trust before pitch
