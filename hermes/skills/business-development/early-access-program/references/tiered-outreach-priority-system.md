# Tiered Outreach Priority System

## Overview

Classify beta targets into three tiers based on readiness, accessibility, and pain acuity. This prevents wasting effort on cold enterprise prospects when warm single-location targets are available.

## Tier Definitions

### Tier 1: Immediate Outreach
**Criteria:**
- Pain is ACUTE and VISIBLE (20+ units aged 60+ days, public complaints)
- Decision maker is DIRECTLY ACCESSIBLE (GM/owner, not buried in org chart)
- Single location or small group (2-3 stores) — no corporate approval chains
- Located in high-pain markets (extreme climate, oversupply, seasonal pressure)
- Active on LinkedIn or responsive via phone/email

**Approach:** Direct contact within 48 hours. Phone call or personalized LinkedIn message referencing specific pain.

**Example from LotSignal:**
- Camelback Ford (Phoenix, AZ) — extreme heat accelerates inventory aging
- Freeman Toyota (Dallas, TX) — independent culture, direct communicators
- OffLeaseOnly (Orlando, FL) — used-car specialist with 500 units

### Tier 2: Warm Outreach
**Criteria:**
- Pain exists but may be managed by current processes
- Multi-location small groups (3-10 stores) — some approval needed
- Located in target markets but less saturated
- Referred or connected through network
- Active on social but not publicly complaining

**Approach:** 2-week nurture sequence before asking. Share value content, comment on posts, build relationship.

**Example from LotSignal:**
- Hendrick Honda (Charlotte, NC) — corporate group but local GM autonomy
- Classic Chevrolet (Sugar Land, TX) — suburban market, family buyers

### Tier 3: Strategic Outreach
**Criteria:**
- Large enterprise or franchise groups (10+ locations)
- Currently have competing solutions in place
- Long sales cycles (6+ months)
- Require procurement/RFP processes
- High potential value but low immediacy

**Approach:** Case study and proof-point cultivation. Target AFTER Tier 1/2 successes. Use as reference accounts.

**Example from LotSignal:**
- AutoNation (300+ locations) — enterprise play after local proof
- CarMax (240+ locations) — will never beta test, but good reference target

## Scoring Matrix

| Factor | Weight | Tier 1 Score | Tier 2 Score | Tier 3 Score |
|--------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| Pain acuity | 3x | 4-5 | 2-3 | 1-2 |
| Decision speed | 2x | 4-5 | 2-3 | 1 |
| Market timing | 2x | 4-5 | 3 | 2 |
| Accessibility | 1x | 5 | 3 | 2 |
| **Weighted Total** | | **32-40** | **18-26** | **8-13** |

**Rule:** Only pursue Tier 3 after closing 3+ Tier 1 deals.
