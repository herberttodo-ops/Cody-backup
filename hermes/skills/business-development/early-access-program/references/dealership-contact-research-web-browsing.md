# Dealership Contact Research via Web Browsing

## When to Use
When you need to find GM/owner contact info for car dealerships and cannot access LinkedIn Sales Navigator or paid tools.

## Technique

### Step 1: Find Dealership Website
- Search: `[dealership name] [city] official website`
- Look for `.com` domains (not third-party listing sites)

### Step 2: Navigate to Staff/Team Page
- Common paths: `/staff`, `/team`, `/about`, `/contact`
- Look for "Meet Our Team", "Staff", "Management"

### Step 3: Extract Contact Info
- **Phone**: Usually on contact page or footer
- **Address**: For local market context
- **GM Name**: Staff page often lists General Manager
- **LinkedIn**: Search `[GM name] [dealership name]` on LinkedIn

### Step 4: Verify via Google Maps
- Search dealership name
- Confirm phone number matches website
- Check business hours for call timing

## Example: Camelback Ford (Phoenix, AZ)
- Website: camelbackford.com
- Phone: 844-873-7964 (from contact page)
- Address: 1330 E. Camelback Road, Phoenix, AZ 85014
- Staff page: camelbackford.com/staff (GM: Chris Hough)

## Notes
- Family-owned dealers often list owner names directly
- Corporate dealers (AutoNation) may only show local GM
- Call during business hours (9 AM - 5 PM local time)
- Have your 30-second pitch ready before calling
