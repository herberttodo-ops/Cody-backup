# OptiRFP Content Strategy v2.0

## The Problem
Current content was repetitive: 7 headlines (many duplicates) + 3 body templates = limited variety and predictable posting pattern.

## The Solution
7 distinct content pillars with 48+ unique headlines and 12 different body angles, ensuring variety while maintaining brand voice.

## Body Template Rotation

### Template 1: The Evaluator's Perspective
"#RFP evaluators have seen it all. Generic claims, recycled language, obvious templates.

The winners? They prove every claim. Specific metrics. Real outcomes. Names you can verify.

Stand out by standing up to scrutiny."

**Best for:** Pillar 1 (Problem), Pillar 6 (Action)

### Template 2: The So-What Framework
"Most RFP responses bury the value under features.

Flip it. Lead with outcomes. Lead with proof. Lead with the transformation they actually care about.

The 'so what' is what wins."

**Best for:** Pillar 2 (Solution), Pillar 5 (Contrarian)

### Template 3: The Hook
"The evaluators made their decision on page one. Whether they knew it or not.

Your first impression is your only impression. Make it count.

Lead with impact. Mirror their priorities. Make them want page two."

**Best for:** Pillar 2 (Solution), Pillar 4 (Metrics)

### Template 4: The Mirror Strategy
"People trust what looks familiar. It's cognitive bias, and it's your advantage.

Mirror their terminology. Reflect their priorities. Use their language like it was your first.

The closer you match their mental model, the easier you are to select."

**Best for:** Pillar 3 (Psychology), Pillar 2 (Solution)

### Template 5: The Anti-Template
"Templates save time. They also scream 'bulk send.'

Every boilerplate paragraph is a signal: This wasn't written for you.

Cut the generic. Add the specific. One custom detail outweighs ten recycled features."

**Best for:** Pillar 1 (Problem), Pillar 6 (Action)

### Template 6: The Specificity Principle
"'Best-in-class' means nothing. '20% faster' means something. '20% faster validated by ClientCo' means everything.

Specificity builds credibility. Numbers build trust. Names build confidence.

Generic claims don't win competitive RFPs."

**Best for:** Pillar 4 (Metrics), Pillar 3 (Psychology)

### Template 7: The Process Insights
"Winning RFP responses aren't written. They're engineered.

Each section answers a specific question. Each paragraph moves the evaluator forward. Each sentence earns the next.

Structure is strategy."

**Best for:** Pillar 2 (Solution), Pillar 5 (Contrarian)

### Template 8: The Time Reality
"Your evaluator has 20 proposals and 2 hours.

They're not reading. They're scanning. They're pattern-matching. They're deciding.

Make the decision easy. Put the win where they'll see it first."

**Best for:** Pillar 3 (Psychology), Pillar 4 (Metrics)

### Template 9: The AI Warning
"AI-generated responses all sound the same. Confident, polished, and hollow.

Evaluators feel it. They can't quite say why, but they know.

Human insight beats algorithmic fluency every time."

**Best for:** Pillar 1 (Problem), Pillar 5 (Contrarian)

### Template 10: The Questioning Mindset
"Good RFP responses answer the questions asked.

Winning RFP responses answer the questions behind the questions. The unspoken concerns. The hidden priorities. The real decision criteria.

Read between the lines."

**Best for:** Pillar 7 (Questions), Pillar 5 (Contrarian)

### Template 11: The Confidence Play
"Every hedging phrase weakens your position.

'May help' → 'Reduces'
'Can potentially' → 'Delivers'
'We believe' → 'Proven by'

Confidence builds credibility. Tentativeness telegraphs uncertainty."

**Best for:** Pillar 6 (Action), Pillar 3 (Psychology)

### Template 12: The Curation Principle
"Not every RFP is worth your time. Not every bid deserves a response.

The best teams say no more often than yes. They know their win rates by industry, by size, by deadline.

Strategy starts with selection."

**Best for:** Pillar 5 (Contrarian), Pillar 6 (Action)

### Auto-Generation Logic
1. Select random headline from expanded pool
2. Select random body (can add pillar matching in v2.1)
3. Select random hashtag set
4. Generate with appropriate topic for visuals

### Quality Control
- Keep headline length: 5-8 words optimal
- Topic matching ensures appropriate visual
- Generate 3x options, pick best via vision analysis

## Maintenance

### Add New Content Every Month
- 5-10 new headlines
- 2-3 new body templates
- Monitor performance by pillar

### Deprecate Underperformers
- Track engagement by headline/body combo
- Remove consistently low performers
- Keep variety in winning pillars

### Seasonal Themes
- Q1: New year, new approach to RFPs
- Q2: Mid-year proposal health check
- Q3: Budget season prep
- Q4: Planning for next year's wins

---

## Success Metrics

- **Variety Score**: 10+ unique headlines before repeats → Target: 30 days
- **Engagement Rate**: Track per-pillar performance
- **Visual Variety**: Topic diversity (copy-paste, winning, specificity, etc.)
- **Feed Appearance**: No adjacent posts should feel similar
