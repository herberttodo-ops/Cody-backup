# Automotive Dealership Marketing Strategy

Complete social media strategy for automotive dealership software companies like LotSignal.

## Market Context

**Product Type:** Automotive marketing software for car dealerships  
**Core Value Props:** Inventory management, lead generation, customer retention, marketing automation  
**Target Buyers:** Dealership owners, General Sales Managers, Marketing Managers, BDC Managers  
**Competitive Landscape:** Dealer.com, Cars.com, AutoTrader, vAuto, Dealertrack

## 8 Core Dealership Pain Points

1. **Inventory aging** — Units sitting too long killing floorplan
2. **Lead quality** — Internet leads converting at 8-12%
3. **Marketing attribution** — Can't prove ROI on ad spend
4. **Customer retention** — 70%+ of sales go to previous owners, hard to re-engage
5. **Price compression** — Competing on price, not value
6. **Staff turnover** — BDC reps burning out, hard to train
7. **OEM compliance** — Co-op requirements, approved messaging
8. **Time starvation** — GSMs wearing 5 hats, no time to optimize

---

## 4 Buyer Personas

### 1. Dealership Owner / GM
- **Demographics:** 45-65, owns 1-5 rooftops
- **Daily pain:** Cash flow, floorplan costs, staff headaches
- **Cares about:** ROI, net profit, exit strategy
- **Where they hang:** LinkedIn, industry podcasts, NADA events
- **Content that works:** Profit-focused case studies, peer dealer wins

### 2. General Sales Manager (GSM)
- **Demographics:** 35-50, manages floor + internet teams
- **Daily pain:** Hitting monthly numbers, CRM adoption, lead follow-up
- **Cares about:** Units sold, gross per deal, team performance
- **Where they hang:** Facebook groups, 20-groups, vendor webinars
- **Content that works:** Sales tactics, CRM workflows, objection handling

### 3. Marketing Manager
- **Demographics:** 28-45, may wear multiple hats
- **Daily pain:** Budget battles, proving attribution, content creation
- **Cares about:** Cost per lead, social engagement, brand consistency
- **Where they hang:** Instagram, TikTok, marketing Slack groups
- **Content that works:** Creative examples, time-saving tools, viral wins

### 4. BDC Manager
- **Demographics:** 25-40, manages phone/email/internet teams
- **Daily pain:** Rep turnover, script adherence, lead response time
- **Cares about:** Contact rate, appointment set rate, show rate
- **Where they hang:** Facebook, TikTok, call center forums
- **Content that works:** Phone scripts, objection handling, motivation

## Content Types by Platform

### Facebook (Dealer-focused B2B)
- **What works:** Video testimonials, quick tips, industry memes
- **Format:** 30-90 sec videos, carousel posts with data
- **Best times:** 8am, 12pm, 5pm local dealer time
- **Hashtag strategy:** #automotive #dealership #carbiz #leadership

### Instagram (Visual + Reels)
- **What works:** Before/after inventory shots, Reels with trending audio
- **Format:** 9:16 Reels (7-15 sec), Stories with polls
- **Best times:** 11am, 2pm, 7pm
- **Hashtag strategy:** #cars #dealershiplife #automotive #carculture

### LinkedIn (Owner/GSM focused)
- **What works:** Thought leadership, dealer spotlights, industry data
- **Format:** Text posts with line breaks, PDF carousels, video
- **Best times:** 7-9am, 12pm, 5-6pm
- **Hashtag strategy:** #automotive #dealership #leadership #b2b

### TikTok (BDC + frontline staff)
- **What works:** Quick tips, day-in-the-life, viral trends with car twist
- **Format:** 15-60 sec vertical, trending sounds
- **Best times:** 12pm, 4pm, 7-9pm
- **Hashtag strategy:** #cars #dealership #cartok #automotive

### YouTube (Long-form education)
- **What works:** Product demos, dealer interviews, how-to guides
- **Format:** 5-15 min tutorials, Shorts for clips
- **Best times:** Publish Tue-Thu, promote on other platforms
- **SEO:** Target "how to [problem] dealership" keywords

## Research Process

When building a social media plan for automotive clients:

1. **Competitor audit** — Analyze Dealer.com, vAuto, Cars.com social presence
2. **Dealer interviews** — 3-5 calls with target persona dealers
3. **Trend analysis** — Google Trends, TikTok Creative Center, LinkedIn analytics
4. **Content audit** — What's working for auto SaaS on each platform

Use parallel subagents for research: one per competitor, one per buyer persona, one per platform.
