# B2B Building in Public Social Media Strategy
## Authentic Personal + Professional Authority Content Mix

## Overview

For B2B SaaS products (OptiRFP, LotSignal), the highest-engagement strategy combines:
- **Business Page**: Industry stats, customer wins, product features
- **Personal Profile**: Dealer conversations, feature failures, honest struggles

This creates asymmetric trust: the business page builds authority, the personal profile builds relationship.

---

## Content Mix Ratio

| Channel | Tone | Posting Days | Time | Content Type |
|---------|------|-------------|------|--------------|
| **Business Page** | Professional authority | Mon-Fri | 8:00 AM EST | Industry stats, tips, wins |
| **Personal Profile** | Humble, authentic | Tue/Thu/Sat | 7:00 PM EST | Behind-scenes, struggles, lessons |

---

## Personal Post Templates (Humble/Early-Stage)

### 1. "I Was Wrong" Format
```
I built the wrong thing. Twice.

V1: [what you built] → [why it failed]
V2: [what you built] → [why it failed]
V3: [what you're shipping now]

The lesson: [insight]
```

### 2. "Customer Quote" Format
```
Just hung up with [title] in [location].

They said something that's haunting me:
"[direct quote]"

Think about that. [reflection]

Here's what broke my brain: [insight]

This is why I'm doing this.
```

### 3. "Three Things I Got Wrong" Format
```
Three months ago, I thought [assumption].

I was wrong.

❌ [Wrong assumption 1]
✅ [What I found instead]

❌ [Wrong assumption 2]
✅ [What I found instead]

❌ [Wrong assumption 3]
✅ [What I found instead]

The aha moment: [insight]
```

### 4. "The Messy Middle" Format
```
[Specific operational detail showing work-in-progress]

Not polished. Not triumphant. Just honest.

[What you're still figuring out]
```

---

## Tone Rules

| Instead of: | Use: |
|-------------|------|
| "I solved it" | "I was solving the wrong thing" |
| "Dealers need X" | "I thought dealers needed X, but..." |
| "Here's the answer" | "Here's what I'm learning" |
| "We built" | "I missed / I assumed / I found" |
| "Scale fast" | "Might take 3 years, or not work at all" |
| "Revolutionary" | "Trying to fix one small thing" |

## Graphic Design for B2B

### Personal Posts (Humble)
- **Colors**: Steel gray, navy blue, white, muted tones
- **Style**: Minimalist, contemplative, not flashy
- **Elements**: Question marks, timelines, before/after comparisons
- **Mood**: Work-in-progress, authentic, reflective

### Business Posts (Authority)
- **Colors**: Navy blue (#1e3a5f), alert orange, steel gray
- **Style**: Industrial, professional, data-driven
- **Elements**: Car silhouettes, lot aerials, dashboard mockups, counters
- **Mood**: Confident but not arrogant

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Business Page | Personal Profile |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| **Engagement Rate** | 1-3% (industry norm) | 3-8% (authenticity premium) |
| **Follower Growth** | Slow, steady | Faster with viral posts |
| **Lead Quality** | High intent, lower volume | Medium intent, higher volume |
| **Conversion Path** | Direct to product | To personal DM → to product |
