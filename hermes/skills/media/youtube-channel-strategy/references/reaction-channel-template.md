## The Reaction Channel Advantage

1. **Low production cost** - No scripting, minimal editing
2. **Evergreen opportunity** - New releases weekly
3. **Built-in audience** - Fans of the content reacting to
4. **Algorithm-friendly** - Trending topics = suggested traffic

## Title Templates

### Formula 1: "First Time + Emotion"
```
[MOVIE TITLE] REACTION | First Time Watching [Year] | [Emotion]
```
Example: "PULP FICTION REACTION | First Time Watching [2024] | I get the hype now..."

### Formula 2: "The Question Hook"
```
[MOVIE TITLE] REACTION | Was it worth the wait? [Year]
```
Example: "OPPENHEIMER REACTION | Was it worth the wait?"

### Formula 3: "Direct Statement"
```
[MOVIE TITLE] REACTION | [Statement hinting at reaction]
```
Example: "DEADPOOL 3 TRAILER REACTION | They actually brought him back..."

## Thumbnail Templates

### Template 1: "Split Face" (Best for Classics)
- **Left:** Normal/excited expression
- **Right:** Shocked/emotional expression  
- **Overlay:** Movie poster
- **Text:** "FIRST TIME" or emotion word

### Template 2: "The Reaction Shot" (Best for Trailers)
- **Center:** Your face in utter shock
- **Element:** Red arrow pointing to face
- **Text:** "THEY DIDN'T..." or "WAIT..."

### Template 3: Before & After (Best for Full Movies)
- **Left:** Smiling before
- **Arrow:** →
- **Right:** Distressed after
- **Text:** "EMOTIONAL"

## The Retention Toolkit

### First 30 Seconds
- Promise something incredible is coming
- "The twist at 15:30 will blow your mind"
- Use pattern interrupts (angle changes)

### Middle Content
- Segment markers: "At 12:34 something CRAZY happens"
- Pattern interrupt every 2-3 minutes
- Visual variety (B-roll, zoom on face)

### End Screen
- Link 2 related reactions
- "Watch my [Franchise] reaction next"
- Pin comment: "What should I react to next?"

## What NOT To Do

❌ React to everything (stay focused on movies/film)
❌ Skip SEO (titles/descriptions matter hugely)
❌ Long gaps between uploads (algorithm punishes)
❌ Ignore Shorts (they drive massive discovery)
❌ Spoilers in titles (kills click-through)
