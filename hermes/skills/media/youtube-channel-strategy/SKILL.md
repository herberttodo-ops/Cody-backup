# YouTube Channel Strategy

## When to Use

Use when the user asks for:
- Channel growth analysis and recommendations
- Monetization roadmaps (YPP, revenue streams)
- Content calendars and posting schedules
- Title/thumbnail optimization strategies
- Niche-specific tactics (reactions, education, vlogging, etc.)
- Audience building and retention strategies
- Competitive analysis in their space

## Overview

This skill provides strategic consulting for YouTube creators, covering the full lifecycle from launch to monetization to growth phases.

## The Strategic Framework

### Phase 1: Foundation (0-1K subs)
**Goal:** Establish consistency and algorithm trust

**Key Actions:**
- Post 2-3x/week minimum
- Focus on searchable, trending content
- Reply to EVERY comment (signals engagement)
- Create 5-10 binge-worthy playlists
- Establish thumbnail/title consistency

**Metrics to Track:**
- CTR (click-through rate) > 5%
- AVD (average view duration) > 40%
- Subscribers per video trend
- Traffic sources (Search, Suggested, Browse)

### Phase 2: Momentum (1K-10K subs)
**Goal:** Find viral potential and double down

**Key Actions:**
- Add Shorts (algorithm boost)
- Go LIVE for trending moments
- Collaborate with similar-size creators
- Create series/recurring segments
- Community posts 2x/week

### Phase 3: Monetization & Scale (10K+ subs)
**Goal:** Diversify revenue and build team

**Key Actions:**
- YPP + Memberships + Super Thanks
- Affiliate marketing integration
- Sponsorship outreach
- Off-platform presence (Patreon, merch)
- Delegation/editing help

## Content Strategy by Niche

### Reaction Channels
**Hook:** "First time watching [X]"
**Timing:** Within 2 hours of trailer drops
**Series:** Chronological franchise watches, "I missed this as a kid"
**Thumbnails:** Split face (before/after emotion)

### Educational Channels
**Hook:** "Learn [skill] in [timeframe]"
**Timing:** Evergreen + trending topic tie-ins
**Series:** Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced progression
**Thumbnails:** Clear value prop + your face as authority

### Vlogging/Lifestyle
**Hook:** Day-in-the-life with stakes
**Timing:** Consistent posting schedule
**Series:** Weekly rituals, seasonal content
**Thumbnails": Bright, personal, curiosity gap

## Title Formulas That Work

### Formula 1: "First Time + Emotion"
```
[CONTENT] REACTION | First Time [Year] | [Emotion]
```
Example: "PULP FICTION REACTION | First Time [2024] | I get the hype"

### Formula 2: "The Question Hook"
```
[CONTENT] REACTION | Was it worth the wait? [Year]
```
Example: "OPPENHEIMER REACTION | Was it worth the wait?"

### Formula 3: "Direct Statement"
```
[CONTENT] REACTION | [Statement hinting at reaction]
```
Example: "DEADPOOL 3 TRAILER | They actually brought him back..."

### Formula 4: "Series Template"
```
[Series] REACTION | [Episode] | My Journey Continues [Year]
```
Example: "MCU REACTION | Iron Man | My Marvel Journey Begins"

## Thumbnail Psychology

### High-CTR Elements
1. **Your face with emotion** - Humans look at faces first
2. **Split composition** - Before/after, expectation vs reality
3. **Text overlay** - Readable at small sizes (< 60 chars)
4. **Red arrows/circles** - Direct attention (sparingly)
5. **Color contrast** - Stand out in crowded feeds

### Color Psychology
| Color | Use Case |
|-------|----------|
| Red | Urgency, excitement, trailers |
| Yellow | Attention, optimism, comedy |
| Black | Mystery, drama, suspense |
| Blue | Trust, education, analysis |
| White | Readability, clean aesthetic |

## The Retention Toolkit

### First 30 Seconds (Critical)
- Promise something incredible is coming
- Use pattern interrupts (angle changes, zooms)
- Preview the emotional journey

### Middle (The "Valley")
- Segment markers: "At 12:34 something CRAZY happens"
- Pattern interrupts every 2-3 minutes
- Visual variety (B-roll, graphics, face)

### End Screen Strategy
- Link 2 related videos (binge loop)
- CTA: "Comment [specific prompt]"
- Subscribe pitch tied to series

## Monetization Math

### YouTube Partner Program Requirements
- 1,000 subscribers
- 4,000 public watch hours (or 10M Shorts views)
- No active Community Guidelines strikes
- 2FA enabled

### Revenue Streams (Build in Order)
1. **YPP Ads** - $2-10 RPM typically
2. **Affiliate Marketing** - Amazon, product links
3. **Channel Memberships** - $4.99/mo tiers
4. **Super Features** - Thanks during Lives
5. **Sponsorships** - $500-5K per deal (10K+ subs)
6. **Off-platform** - Patreon, courses, merch

## Weekly Content Calendar Template

```
Monday:    Evergreen content (searchable)
Tuesday:   Trending/timely content
Wednesday: Community building (polls, Shorts)
Thursday:  Series installment
Friday:    Event content (releases, premieres)
Saturday:  Behind-the-scenes/vlog
Sunday:     Planning/batching for next week
```

**Optimal Posting Times (Eastern):**
- Weekdays: 2-4 PM (after school/work)
- Weekends: 9-11 AM (morning browsing)
- Trending moments: ASAP (within hours)

## SEO Checklist

- [ ] Chapters for videos > 8 minutes

## Analytics Deep Dive

### Metrics That Matter (In Order)
1. **CTR** - Are thumbnails/titles working?
2. **AVD** - Are you delivering on the promise?
3. **Returning vs New** - Audience loyalty
4. **Traffic Sources** - Where discovery happens
5. **Subscribers from Video** - Conversion rate

### Red Flags
- CTR < 3% (thumbnail/title problem)
- AVD < 30% (content mismatch with expectation)
- Traffic dominated by one source (algorithm risk)
- High impressions, low click (clickbait suspicion)

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Inconsistency** - Algorithm punishes gaps
2. **Chasing trends only** - No searchable library
3. **Ignoring Shorts** - Massive discoverability
4. **Poor mobile optimization** - 70%+ watch mobile
5. **No series/playlist strategy** - Miss binges
6. **Engagement in first hour** - Reply to comments fast
7. **No off-platform presence** - Platform risk

## Session Notes

When analyzing a channel, always ask:
1. What's the specific niche?
2. Current subscriber count and goal?
3. What's working already? (replicate)
4. What's the monetization timeline?
5. Batch filming possible?

Provide actionable, specific advice, not generic platitudes. Include numbers, timelines, and checklists.
